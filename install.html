Setup help

Start with
your kit.

Unzip the Founders’ Brain kit you received and open 1. START-HERE.html. Choose Claude Cowork or Codex, then follow its checklist: installation, technical setup, and onboarding when you’re ready.

Keep the kit’s files together. Start Here contains the installation instructions and copy-and-paste prompts for your included plugins. Keep that guide open as you work through the steps.

What you’ll need

  • Your client kit

    If you haven’t received it or can’t find it, message me for your copy.

  • Claude Cowork or Codex

    Use the desktop app and an account with access to plugins and the Notion connection. You only need the app you plan to use.

  • Your own Notion workspace

    You can start with Notion Free; Notion AI is not required. The kit guides you through duplicating the template and connecting your assistant to your copy.

If you get stuck

  • A plugin isn’t showing up

    Check the installation steps for your selected app in Start Here, including any restart, then open a fresh task.

  • Your assistant can’t find the workspace

    Check that you supplied your own Notion copy’s link and that the connection has access to it.

  • Setup stopped halfway through

    Keep the conversation and ask your assistant to continue from the last completed step. Use the same Notion copy.

Already using Founders’ Brain? Keep your existing workspace and follow Help & updates in the kit before making changes.
