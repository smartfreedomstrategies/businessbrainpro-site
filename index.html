What it does with that memory

The Command Center.
It listens. It acts. It reports.

It listens. You just talk.

For example: “Here are my meeting notes. Save the decisions and my next action.” Your assistant checks the details, saves the recap and links the task. Later, ask what’s due and return to that task with the meeting context attached. The next step has a record you can come back to.

It acts on your money.

The money engines read your scored customer list and surface what humans miss: VIPs going cold, buyers ready for the next offer, revenue leaking silently. Each comes with an outreach message already drafted. You always press send.

It reports before you ask.

Choose a morning brief for what's due, what moved and an idea worth revisiting. Add the Friday pattern synthesis and weekly recap if you want them. Setup confirms the times, permissions and tools needed to run them.

It learns your business.

A guided brand interview teaches it your voice, your customer, your offers and your rules. Then bring the sales history you want to work with. Your information gives the system its context.

Before you write in

The questions everyone asks.

“I tried Notion before, and quit.”
If upkeep was the problem, your assistant can help file, link and update the work you bring it. You still supply the context and review the results. Some trackers are manual, and a health check can flag structural issues that need attention.
“Doesn't ChatGPT already do this?”
The Brain adds shared business records in your own Notion and workflows for using them. Brand onboarding supplies your voice, offers, customers and rules, so your assistant can work from the same context across sessions.
“I don't have time to implement anything.”
You don't build it from scratch, I do the heavy lifting. But yes, you need a few hours with me, on your real business: you bring the context (site, products, customers, how you work), I set it into the system, over a few guided sessions. Because it's your business, it can't happen without you.
“What if an update breaks something?”
Use the update preview, back up your workspace and keep the previous plugin files. The kit includes update and recovery guidance. Reinstalling an older plugin does not reverse changes already made to Notion.
“How much does it cost?”
I don't work with fixed packages. Let's talk for 15-20 minutes about your business. If it's a fit, we agree which plugins and workflows are included, the setup and onboarding work, and any support after handover before you commit. The quote covers that scope. If it's not a fit, I'll tell you.
“What tools do I have to pay for?”
Use Claude Cowork or Codex with an account that supports plugins and the Notion connection. You can start with Notion Free; Notion AI is not required. Your AI provider’s usage limits still apply. Check your chosen provider’s current plan before subscribing. Your kit’s Start Here guide covers both routes. Visit setup help if you need a hand.
“What happens to my data if I stop?”
Your saved records stay in your own Notion workspace. Stopping the plugins does not remove those records.
